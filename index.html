What do I do?

I help companies get products out of their heads and into the world. I take your Big Idea or Big Problem, analyze it, and turn it into executable bites strategically designed to reach your specific goals. I believe an experience-driven, user-focused approach is the foundation of a successful product.

Often companies jump straight from an idea into design and development without strategically defining their product and how it might best balance the needs of the business with the needs of their target users. This leads to waste of time, money, and effort. That's where I come in.

My goal is to take your vision for your product, balance the user and business needs, and design an optimal solution for your particular business that is ready for design and development. Your product strategy will be designed for the real world.
