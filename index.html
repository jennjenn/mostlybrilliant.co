What do I do?

I help companies get products out of their heads and into the world. I take your Big Idea or Big Problem, analyze it, and turn it into executable bites strategically designed to reach your specific goals. I believe an experience-driven, user-focused approach is the foundation of a successful product.

Often companies jump straight from an idea into design and development without strategically defining their product and how it might best balance the needs of the business with the needs of their target users. This leads to waste of time, money, and effort. That's where I come in.

My goal is to take your vision for your product, balance the user and business needs, and design an optimal solution for your particular business that is ready for design and development. Your product strategy will be designed for the real world.

How the magic happens

Currently I provide two services, one designed for brand new companies, and the other for existing products looking for their next best steps.

From Scratch is designed for that idea that needs to be molded into a product to built and sent out into the world. I'll get you from your lofty vision to the brass tacks it'll take to get it built. When we're done you'll have a product plan, wireframes, a clickable wireframe prototype, and a full set of product requirements in the form of a user journey. More importantly, you'll have your vision translated into the tactical language of designers and engineers who can take it and turn it into reality. I'll even help you staff up design and development through my friends at Gun.io. Starts at $6500.

Next Best Steps. Congrats! Your product is out in the world! But what's next? You have a to do list a mile long, but don't know the best place to start. I'll do a full audit of your product. I'll review your roadmap, metrics, user feedback, and any other datapoints you have. If needed, I'll set up user feedback calls, surveys, and additional tracking systems. From this, you'll get a report of at least 15 opportunities for quick improvement and optimization. I'll suggest product features, updates, and prioritization adjustments for the next 3-6 months based on the data and feedback. We'll have a 1 hour call following my report where you can ask any burning questions. $1500 for up to 5 key views/screens.
